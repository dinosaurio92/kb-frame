# [Project Name] — Decisions

> History, read only when the task needs it. `Genesis.md` holds the state and the open decisions.
> Every closed decision records **why**, **what was rejected** and **what would revert it**. Agents remember *what* was decided; they forget *why* and repeat the rejected option. These two columns prevent that.

## Closed decisions

Not reopened without a reason that is new. Newest last.

| Date | Decision | Why | Rejected | Reverts if |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| YYYY-MM-DD | **Choice made**, in one line | The reason that carried it | Option B (why not), option C (why not) | The evidence or event that would reopen it |

## Dead ends

Things tried and dropped. One line each, so nobody tries them again without knowing the cost.

| Date | Tried | Result | Do not retry unless |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| YYYY-MM-DD | What was attempted | Why it failed, with the number or error | What has to be different first |
